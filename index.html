Our story

From Shenaky to California.

Shenaky Winery was inspired by my birthplace, the village of Shenaky in the Istalif district, north of Kabul.

My journey later passed through France and ultimately reached California, where I began making wine in 2009. We believe good wine is the result of time, experience, and a commitment to quality.

Un vin qui pleure est un bon vin.
