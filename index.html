Our Story

A journey shaped by vineyards, France, and California.

Shenaky Winery begins with a journey rather than a business plan. Its inspiration reaches back to Shenaky, a village in the historic district of Istalif, a region long associated with vineyards, orchards, and mountain landscapes.

Years spent in France deepened the founder’s appreciation for wine as culture, hospitality, family, and tradition. California later became the place where those influences came together through winemaking that began in 2009.

Three Influences
