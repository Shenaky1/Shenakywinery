Wine Guide

How to Enjoy Our Wines

Simple serving suggestions, food pairings, and signature ways to enjoy each Shenaky wine.

2024

Red Blend

Typical character: Dark fruit, gentle spice, and a smooth finish.

Serve slightly cool at 60 to 65°F in a Bordeaux or universal wine glass.

Enjoy with
Beef, burgers, or barbecue.
Serving idea
Open 20 minutes before serving.
Choose Red Blend
2024 and 2025

Merlot

Typical character: Plum and black cherry with a soft, rounded finish.

Serve slightly cool at 60 to 65°F in a Bordeaux or universal wine glass.

Enjoy with
Beef, lamb, pasta, or mushrooms.
Serving idea
Let the wine breathe for 15 minutes.
Choose Merlot
2024

Sauvignon Blanc

Typical character: Citrus and green apple with a crisp finish.

Serve chilled at 45 to 50°F in a white wine glass.

Enjoy with
Seafood, salads, or goat cheese.
Serving idea
Keep chilled and serve fresh.
Choose Sauvignon Blanc
2025

Symphony

Typical character: Floral aromas and ripe peach with a fresh finish.

Serve chilled at 45 to 50°F in an aromatic white wine glass.

Enjoy with
Spicy dishes, poultry, or soft cheese.
Serving idea
Serve as an aperitif or with a light meal.
Choose Symphony
2023 · 375 ml · 12.6% ABV

Riesling Ice Wine

Typical character: Concentrated sweetness with apricot and honeyed notes.

Serve well chilled at 42 to 46°F in a small dessert wine glass. A 2 oz pour is ideal.

Enjoy with
Dessert, blue cheese, fresh fruit, or on its own.
Serving idea
Pour slowly and savor in small sips.
Choose Riesling Ice Wine
