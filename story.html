Our Story

From Shenaky to California.

Shenaky Winery was inspired by my birthplace, the village of Shenaky in the Istalif district, north of Kabul.

A personal journey expressed through small-batch winemaking.

Years spent in France deepened my appreciation for wine as part of culture, family, hospitality, and everyday life.

California gave me the opportunity to bring those influences together. Since 2009, I have continued to learn, refine my methods, and make wine in limited quantities with care and patience.

Un vin qui pleure est un bon vin.A wine that weeps is a good wine.
